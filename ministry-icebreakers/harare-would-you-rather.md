# Would You Rather — Harare Edition 🇿🇼
*Clean, family-friendly, and just controversial enough to start an argument (in love).*

---

## 🍲 Food Fights (the most dangerous category)

1. Would you rather eat **sadza with a fork and knife** in front of everyone at church lunch, OR eat **rice with your hands** at a fancy Borrowdale wedding?
2. Would you rather give up **Sunday rice and chicken** forever, OR give up **sadza** for one whole month?
3. Would you rather eat **madora (mopane worms)** every day for a week, OR never eat **nyama** again at a braai?
4. Would you rather only drink **Mazoe Orange Crush** made way too weak by Gogo, OR made way too strong by your little cousin?
5. Would you rather be the one who **finishes the last piece of huku** at a family gathering, OR be the one everyone saw **looking at it** for 10 minutes?
6. Would you rather have **muriwo une dovi** at every meal, OR **matemba (kapenta)** at every meal?
7. Would you rather eat **road runner (huku yechivanhu)** that takes 4 hours to chew, OR **broiler chicken** that disappears in 4 seconds?
8. Would you rather your plate at a wedding have **too much rice and one small piece of meat**, OR **lots of meat but no relish/gravy**?
9. Would you rather only eat **maputi** for snacks forever, OR only **freezits**?
10. Would you rather make sadza for **200 people at a church conference**, OR wash all **200 plates** after?

---

## 🚐 Kombi & Town Life

11. Would you rather sit **squeezed in as the 5th person on a 4-person kombi seat**, OR sit on the **"conductor's lap" seat** by the door for the whole ride?
12. Would you rather ride a **mushikashika** that drives like a Formula 1 car, OR a **ZUPCO** that stops at every single stop?
13. Would you rather be the **hwindi (conductor) shouting "Town! Town!"** for a day, OR be the person who has to **collect everyone's change** at the back of the kombi?
14. Would you rather get your **change in sweets** at the supermarket, OR have the kombi driver say **"I'll give you your change later"** (and never do)?
15. Would you rather walk from **Fourth Street rank to Copacabana** in the rain with no umbrella, OR walk it in October heat wearing a full suit?
16. Would you rather get lost in **Mbare Musika**, OR get lost in **Sam Levy's Village** with no money?
17. Would you rather be stuck in **Samora Machel Avenue traffic** for 2 hours, OR in a **kombi rank queue** for 2 hours?
18. Would you rather shop only at **mupedzanhamo (the flea market)** for a year, OR only buy clothes from **Edgars/Truworths** but just once a year?

---

## 💡 ZESA, Water & Data Struggles

19. Would you rather have **ZESA for 24 hours but no water**, OR **water for 24 hours but no ZESA**?
20. Would you rather have **unlimited WhatsApp data but no electricity**, OR **unlimited electricity but your data is finished (data yapera)**?
21. Would you rather the power go off **right when you start ironing your Sunday clothes**, OR **right as the pot of sadza starts boiling**?
22. Would you rather carry **20-litre water containers from the borehole** every morning, OR wake up at **3am** because that's the only time the water comes?
23. Would you rather your phone battery die **just before EcoCash confirms payment**, OR right as you're **sending a voice note to Tete**?
24. Would you rather be on **Econet with no airtime**, OR on **NetOne with no network**?
25. Would you rather lose your **EcoCash PIN**, OR lose your **phone charger** during load-shedding season?

---

## 💵 Money Matters (Zim-style)

26. Would you rather get paid in **US dollars that are torn and taped**, OR in **ZiG that is crisp and new**?
27. Would you rather argue with a **street money changer** about the rate, OR argue with a **vendor** about the price of tomatoes?
28. Would you rather have relatives in the **diaspora** who send money but always ask "What did you do with it?", OR relatives at home who **never ask but always visit at lunchtime**?
29. Would you rather your shopkeeper say **"no change"** every time, OR give you change in **one-dollar notes so old they're soft like cloth**?

---

## 👵🏾 Family & Culture

30. Would you rather be called by **Tete** to "come and sit, we need to talk," OR have **Gogo** call you by **all your siblings' names** before getting yours?
31. Would you rather be the cousin who **always gets sent to the shops**, OR the cousin who **always gets told to wash the dishes**?
32. Would you rather **sit through a 6-hour lobola (roora) negotiation**, OR be the one who has to **cook for everyone** during it?
33. Would you rather your relatives in the **village (kumusha)** think you're rich because you live in Harare, OR your Harare friends think you're "too village"?
34. Would you rather have your mom **post your baby pictures on the family WhatsApp group**, OR have your dad **voice note the whole group a 7-minute message**?
35. Would you rather go **kumusha** for the whole December holiday with no network, OR stay in Harare alone with no ZESA?
36. Would you rather be the person at a **kitchen party** who has to give marriage advice, OR the person who has to **dance in the middle of the circle**?

---

## ⛪ Church Life (laugh at ourselves!)

37. Would you rather the pastor say **"In conclusion…"** three times and keep going, OR say **"I'll be brief"** and preach for 2 hours?
38. Would you rather attend an **all-night prayer (pungwe)** and fall asleep, OR be the one who **gets caught snoring** during a 30-minute prayer?
39. Would you rather lead **praise and worship** with the **keyboard battery dying**, OR lead with the **sound system screaming feedback**?
40. Would you rather be asked to **pray for the food at a big gathering** when everyone is starving, OR be asked to **give a testimony** with no warning?
41. Would you rather sit in the **front row** where the pastor looks at you the whole time, OR the **back row** where the ushers keep asking you to move forward?
42. Would you rather be in charge of **counting the offering coins**, OR in charge of **arranging all the plastic chairs** after the service?
43. Would you rather your phone ring **loudly during prayer** with an old ringtone, OR your stomach rumble **loudly during a quiet moment**?

## ⚽ Big Debates (choose your side!)

44. **Dynamos or CAPS United** — would you rather wear the rival team's jersey for a full week, OR say "my team is the worst" on the family WhatsApp group?
45. Would you rather go to **Lake Chivero** for a church picnic, OR **Mukuvisi Woodlands**?
46. Would you rather live in **Borrowdale with no friends**, OR in **Mbare with all your best friends**?
47. Would you rather have the **jacaranda trees** bloom all year round but leave purple everywhere, OR never see them again?
48. Would you rather **run the Harare Half Marathon**, OR **cook sadza for every runner** who finishes?
49. Would you rather speak **only Shona** for a week, OR **only English** for a week (no mixing allowed — no "ndiri busy"!)?
50. Would you rather be famous for **the best sadza in Harare**, OR **the best voice in the church choir**?

---

## 🎲 How to Play (quick tips)

- **Pick a side:** Read the question aloud. Everyone moves to the **left side of the room for Option A** and **right side for Option B**.
- **Defend it:** Pick one person from each side to give a 30-second "sermon" on why their side is right. Loudest cheer wins.
- **Faith twist (optional):** After the laughter, ask one follow-up — e.g., after #37: *"What's one sermon or verse that stuck with you this year?"* — to turn the fun into real connection.
